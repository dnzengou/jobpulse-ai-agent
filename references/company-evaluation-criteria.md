# Company Evaluation Criteria

Shared reference loaded by company-researcher and resume-customizer agents.

## Research Checklist

For each company, investigate and report on:

### Company Overview
- What does the company do? (one sentence)
- Industry and market position
- Size (employees, revenue if public)
- Founded when, headquartered where
- Key products/services

### Culture and Values
- Stated mission/values on their website
- Glassdoor/Indeed reviews summary (look for patterns, not individual complaints)
- Engineering blog or tech talks (signals engineering culture)
- Remote/hybrid/in-office policy
- Diversity and inclusion signals

### Growth and Stability
- Recent funding rounds or financial news
- Hiring trends (growing, stable, layoffs?)
- Recent product launches or pivots
- Leadership changes

### Tech Stack (for technical roles)
- Languages, frameworks, infrastructure mentioned in job posting
- Tech blog posts or GitHub repos
- Stack signals from job requirements

### Red Flags to Note
- Mass layoffs in recent months
- Glassdoor rating below 3.0 with consistent complaints
- High turnover signals (same role posted repeatedly)
- Vague job descriptions with unrealistic requirements
- "Wear many hats" / "fast-paced" as euphemisms for understaffed
- No clear product-market fit

### Green Flags to Note
- Clear, specific job description
- Salary transparency
- Reasonable requirements matching the level
- Strong engineering blog / open source presence
- Employee tenure data showing retention
- Clear growth path described

## Output Format

Structure the research brief as:

```
# [Company Name] — Research Brief

## Overview
[2-3 sentences: what they do, size, industry]

## The Role
[What the specific job is, team, reporting structure if known]

## Culture & Values
[Key findings from research]

## Tech Stack
[Relevant technology mentioned]

## Growth & Stability
[Financial health, hiring trends, recent news]

## Key Findings
- [Green flag]: ...
- [Green flag]: ...
- [Red flag]: ...

## Resume Customization Notes
[Specific keywords, values, or themes to emphasize when tailoring the resume]
```
