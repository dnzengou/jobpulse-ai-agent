# Engineering Resume Formatting Standards

Authoritative formatting reference for `resume-reviewer`, `resume-builder`, and `resume-customizer` agents. Synthesized from the r/EngineeringResumes wiki and template. Conventions assume **United States and Canada**.

> Two goals: (1) easily **skimmable** by humans, (2) easily **parsable** by ATS. Both are achieved by the same minimalist single-column layout.

---

## Top-Priority Formatting DOs and DON'Ts

| # | DO | DON'T |
|---|----|-------|
| 1 | **Single-column layout.** | Multi-column or sidebar layouts — they break ATS and waste skim space. |
| 2 | **One page**, unless 10+ YoE (rule of thumb: 1 page per decade). | Stretch to 2 pages with low-relevance content. |
| 3 | **Plain-text contact line, no labels.** Email + (optionally) GitHub + portfolio, separated by `|` or similar. | Add `Phone:`, `Email:`, `LinkedIn:`, `Portfolio:`, `GitHub:` prefixes. |
| 4 | **Omit phone, LinkedIn URL, and physical address.** | Add them by default — see best-practices doc. |
| 5 | **Bullets, not paragraphs.** Each bullet 1–2 lines, 1 sentence, no trailing period. | Wall-of-text descriptions, multi-sentence bullets, sub-bullets. |
| 6 | **En dash with surrounding spaces** for date ranges: `Mar 2012 – Mar 2022`. | Hyphens (`-`), the word "to", or no spaces around the en dash. |
| 7 | **Right-align dates to the right margin.** Bullets must not extend past the date column. | Inline dates after job title, or bullets that wrap past the date column. |
| 8 | **Avoid icons, images, graphics, tables, columns, text boxes.** | Decorative resume templates from Canva/Word. |
| 9 | **Modern, easy-to-read font** — Calibri, Bitstream Charter, Arial, Lato, or Helvetica. **Min 10.5pt**, black, full-weight. | Thin/light fonts, gray text, font sizes below 10.5pt. |
| 10 | **Min 0.4-inch margins**, **min 1.07 line spacing**, generous white space between sections. | Cramming the page edge-to-edge. |
| 11 | **Left-aligned text.** | Justified text — causes uneven word spacing and hurts readability. |
| 12 | **Save and submit as PDF** (preserves formatting across systems and platforms). | Submit a `.docx` whose layout shifts on the recruiter's machine. |

## Page Layout

### Length
- **Default: 1 page.** Most engineering resumes do not need a second page once cleaned up.
- **Senior/staff (10+ YoE):** up to 2 pages permitted. Stay under 2.
- **Career changers:** target 1 page.
- A poorly formatted multi-page resume reads worse than a tight 1-pager. The hiring team spends less effort on long resumes — don't punish yourself.

### Margins and spacing
- Vertical and horizontal margins: **minimum 0.4 inches**.
- Line spacing: **minimum 1.07**.
- Consistent and **generous white space** between sections, between jobs/projects, and between lines within a job.
- Clear, visually distinct section breaks.

### Alignment
- Left-aligned body text. **Never justify** — produces inconsistent gaps between words.
- **Right-align dates** to the right margin, on the same line as the role/title.
- No section, no bullet, no piece of content should extend further right than the date column.

### Indentation
- **Don't indent** sections. **Don't indent** bullet points. The bullet glyph itself creates the visual indent already.

---

## Typography

### Font choice
Use one of: **Calibri, Bitstream Charter, Arial, Lato, Helvetica**. Anything modern and easy to read works. Avoid stylized or display fonts.

### Font size
- Body: **10.5pt minimum**.
- Name: larger than body (typical: 14–16pt).
- Verify the font renders well at small sizes and prints clearly in grayscale.

### Color and weight
- **Black text only.** No gray, no color accents.
- Full-weight font — avoid thin/light variants. Verify good contrast against a white background.

### Emphasis (bold, italic, all-caps)
- Use bold, italic, or ALL-CAPS **sparingly** — overuse defeats their purpose (emphasis).
- **Use them independently** of each other, not stacked. Don't bold-and-italicize the same text.
- **Don't bold individual keywords inside bullets** — distracting and unprofessional.
- **Don't italicize bullet text** — italic decreases readability.

### Other
- Don't underline anything in contact info (email, GitHub URL, portfolio).
- Don't color contact links blue or hyperlink-style them visually.

## Contact Header

Place the name as the largest text on the page, with contact details on a single plain-text line directly beneath.

### Per the r/EngineeringResumes template

```
John Doe
name@gmail.com | portfolio.com | github.com/name
```

- Name only — no title underneath, no professional tagline.
- One line of contact details.
- Plain text. No icons. No `Email:`, `Phone:`, `LinkedIn:`, `Portfolio:`, or `GitHub:` labels.
- Strip `https://www.` from URLs.
- No underline, italic, color, or hyperlink visuals.

### Optional add-ins (positioned near the name, only when applicable)
- **Security clearance** (e.g., *"Top Secret / SCI eligible with CI Polygraph"*).
- **Citizenship/work authorization** (e.g., *"US Citizen"*, *"Canadian Citizen"*) — when name or background might raise sponsorship questions.

### Things explicitly omitted from the contact line
- Phone number
- LinkedIn URL
- Physical address / ZIP
- City/state (unless the role is in that exact city)
- More than one email
- A masked email/URL (e.g., `[email](mailto:...)`)

## Dates

### Format rules
- **Use the word "Present"** for ongoing roles. Not *Current*, *Now*, or *Ongoing*.
- **Pick "Month YYYY" or "YYYY"** and stay consistent across the entire resume. If you use months, **use real month names** — not seasons (~~Winter 2022~~) or semesters (~~Spring 2022~~).
- **No specific days** (~~January 1, 2023~~).
- **Full year, not abbreviated**: `2023`, not `'23`.
- **Don't use numeric months**: ~~9/2013~~.
- If abbreviating month names: **Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, June, July, Aug, Sept, Oct, Nov, Dec** — note `June`, `July`, and `Sept` are spelled out (or use `Sept`, not `Sep`). **No periods** after the abbreviation (`Sept`, not `Sept.`).

### Date ranges
- **En dash (–)** with **spaces** on both sides: `Mar 2012 – Mar 2022`.
- Not hyphens (`-`), not "to", and not en dashes without surrounding spaces (`Mar 2012–Mar 2022`).
- **Right-align** the date to the right margin of the page.

### Education dates
- Show the graduation date only — no start date, no range.
- Current student: `Expected May 2025`.
- Graduated: `May 2021` or simply `2021`.

## Section Layout

### Section headers
- Use minimal, consistent styling — typically slightly larger or bold, with a thin rule or extra whitespace below.
- Standard names only: **Experience** (or *Work Experience*), **Education**, **Skills**, **Projects**.
- Don't use creative names: ~~*Professional Experience*, *Selected Experience*, *Technical Skills*, *Personal Projects*, *Where I've Been*~~.

### Order within an entry
**Work Experience entry:**
```
Job Title, Company – City, ST                    Jun 2022 – Present
- Bullet
- Bullet
```

**Education entry:**
```
School – BS in Aerospace Engineering             Apr 2024
```

**Project entry (no employer/dates needed for personal projects):**
```
Project Title                                    github.com/name/project
- Bullet
- Bullet
```

(Per the r/EngineeringResumes template — right-aligned dates and link, left-aligned everything else.)

### Skills section format
- 3 lines or fewer, single column.
- Group by category, one category per line:
  ```
  CAD: Siemens NX, CATIA V5, SolidWorks
  Analysis: Thermal Desktop, Abaqus, LS-DYNA, STAR-CCM+
  ```
- **Comma-separated** within each category. No pipes, hyphens, dashes, or slashes.
- Use proper capitalization for tools: Solid**W**orks, C**ATIA V**5, LabV**IEW**.
- Separate distinct skills: `C, C++` — not `C/C++`.

## Bullet Point Formatting

### Length
- **1–2 lines** per bullet.
- **1 sentence** per bullet — aim for one self-contained idea.
- Don't allow a bullet to wrap to a second line with only 1–4 words on it. Either tighten the bullet or expand it to fill the second line meaningfully.
- Don't allow words to **hyphenate across line breaks**.

### Punctuation and casing
- **No trailing period** at the end of a bullet.
- Avoid apostrophes (`'`), ampersands (`&`), and slashes (`/`) where reasonable.
- **Use digits** for numbers (`8`, not "eight").
- Capitalize only proper nouns. Don't title-case keywords inside bullets.

### Width
- Bullets must **not extend further right than the right-aligned dates** in the section above. Trim or break otherwise.

### Sub-bullets
- Avoid them. They clutter the page and read as padding. If a bullet needs sub-points, the bullet is probably trying to do too much.

## File Naming and Output

### Base resume
`[firstname]-[lastname]-resume.{md,pdf}`
Example: `jon-smith-resume.md`, `jon-smith-resume.pdf`

### Customized resume (per company)
`[firstname]-[lastname]-resume-[company-slug].{md,pdf}`
- Company slug: lowercase, hyphens, no special characters.
- Example: `jon-smith-resume-acme-corp.pdf`.

### Output locations
- Base: `job-search/[firstname]-[lastname]-resume.{md,pdf}`
- Customized: `job-search/[company-slug]/[firstname]-[lastname]-resume-[company-slug].{md,pdf}`

### File format for submission
- **Submit as PDF.** Locks in fonts, spacing, and layout across reviewer machines.
- Keep the markdown source alongside the PDF — it's editable and version-controllable.

## Markdown Source Skeleton

Write the resume as clean markdown that converts cleanly to a PDF following the rules above. **No Professional Summary section by default** (only add for senior/staff, career changers, or to address an employment gap — see best-practices doc).

```
# John Doe
name@gmail.com | portfolio.com | github.com/name

## Skills
CAD: Siemens NX, CATIA V5, SolidWorks
Analysis: Thermal Desktop, Abaqus, LS-DYNA, STAR-CCM+

## Experience

### Job Title, Company – City, ST
*Jun 2022 – Present*

- Past-tense action verb + what was done + measurable result
- Past-tense action verb + what was done + measurable result
- Past-tense action verb + what was done + measurable result

### Job Title, Company – City, ST
*Jan 2021 – May 2022*

- Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]
- Past-tense action verb + what was done + measurable result

## Projects

### Project Title — github.com/name/project

- Real-project bullet, not a tutorial clone
- Real-project bullet, not a tutorial clone

## Education

### School – BS in Aerospace Engineering
*Apr 2024*
```

Notes on the skeleton:
- **No phone number, no LinkedIn URL, no address** in the header — see best-practices doc.
- **No "Professional Summary" / "Objective" section** — see best-practices doc.
- **Section order swaps** based on candidate stage (see best-practices doc): students lead with Education; experienced engineers lead with Experience or Skills; entry-level without work experience leads with Education > Projects > Experience > Skills.
- Use Bachelor **of** Science / Master **of** Science (no apostrophe-s).

## Grammar and Proofreading

- **Spell out obscure abbreviations** the first time used: *"scanning tunneling microscope (STM)"*. Lowercase the spelled-out form unless the term is a proper noun.
- Run **grammar** and **spell** checks. Free tools: Grammarly, Hemingway Editor, LanguageTool, Quillbot.
- Have a human proofread — fresh eyes catch what tools miss.
- Capitalize only **proper nouns** in body text. Don't title-case inside bullets.
- Typos and grammar errors signal carelessness and frequently cause auto-rejection.

## ATS Notes

The single-column, plain-text-link, no-graphics layout above is already ATS-safe. The dominant constraint is **human skimmability**; ATS-safety follows from it. The wiki specifically pushes back against most ATS folklore — modern systems do not auto-reject for font choice or formatting nuances. They store and surface the resume; humans still read it.

What does help with both ATS and humans:
- Standard section names.
- Plain text with no embedded objects.
- Keywords from the job description used naturally inside real bullets — no hidden white-text keyword blocks.
- Submitting a clean PDF.
