# Job Posting Analysis Guide

Shared reference loaded by the company-researcher agent.

## Reading a Job Posting

### Extract the Real Requirements

Job postings often have inflated requirements. Separate:

**Must-haves** (mentioned repeatedly, in the first few bullets):
- Core technical skills for the role
- Years of experience (but treat as flexible — often negotiable)
- Required certifications (if regulated industry)

**Nice-to-haves** (mentioned once, later in the list, prefixed with "preferred" or "bonus"):
- Additional languages/frameworks
- Specific industry experience
- Advanced degrees

**Ignore** (generic filler):
- "Strong communication skills" (everyone lists this)
- "Team player" (meaningless without context)
- "Passionate about technology" (filler)

### Salary Signals

If salary isn't listed:
- Check the company on levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Payscale
- Look at the seniority level and location for market benchmarks
- "Competitive salary" usually means market rate
- If they list a huge range ($80k-$200k), the role level is flexible

### Team and Reporting Structure Clues

- "Report to VP of Engineering" = smaller team, more visibility
- "Join a team of 50+ engineers" = large org, likely specialized
- "Founding engineer" = early stage, broad responsibilities
- "Player-coach" = IC + management hybrid

### Urgency Indicators

- "Immediate start" / "ASAP" = urgent hire, may move fast
- "Building out the team" = new initiative, opportunity to shape
- Reposted multiple times = may have high bar or poor offer

### What to Pull for Resume Customization

From each job posting, extract:
1. **Keywords** — specific technologies, tools, methodologies mentioned
2. **Values language** — what the company emphasizes (innovation, reliability, speed, quality)
3. **Impact language** — how they describe success (scale, revenue, users, efficiency)
4. **Team context** — size, structure, collaboration style
5. **Level signals** — what seniority they actually need based on responsibilities described

These extracted elements feed directly into the resume-customizer agent.
